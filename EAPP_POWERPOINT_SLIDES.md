- 🏅 **Overall** — Pass / Warn / Fail

**Comprehensive Compliance Grid:**

| Regulatory Check | Basis | Typical Status |
|---|---|---|
| NAIC Best Interest (NY Reg 187) | NY DFS | Pass if suitability ≥75 |
| NY Regulation 60 — Replacement | NY DFS | N/A (no replacement) or Pass/Warn |
| NY Regulation 74 — Illustration | NY DFS | Pass when illustration signed |
| OFAC / AML / FinCEN Screening | BSA / FinCEN | Clear |
| IRC §7702 Life Insurance Test | IRS | Pass for standard products |
| IRC §101(a) Tax-Free DB Test | IRS | Pass — standard policy structure |
| IRC §72 Premium / MEC Test | IRS | Pass when within 7-pay limit |
| FINRA Suitability (VUL only) | FINRA | N/A for non-variable; Required for VUL |

**Why Each Check Matters:**

📋 **NAIC Best Interest / NY Reg 187**
Enacted 2019. Requires agents to document why this specific product is in THIS specific client's best interest — not just suitable. AI generates the documentation automatically based on FNA data, financial profile, and product match score.

🔄 **NY Regulation 60 — Replacement**
Protects consumers when replacing existing coverage. Requires a signed disclosure, 15-day notice to prior insurer, and a comparison showing the new policy is superior. AI automatically detects replacement scenarios.

📊 **NY Regulation 74 — Illustration Standards**
Requires policy illustrations to use NAIC-prescribed format. Both agent and client must sign. Illustration must disclose guaranteed vs. non-guaranteed values. AI verifies illustration completeness.

💰 **IRC §7702 — Life Insurance Definition**
A policy must pass either the Cash Value Accumulation Test (CVAT) or the Guideline Premium Test (GPT) to qualify as "life insurance" under IRS rules. Failure means death benefit is taxable — a career-ending error.

🏦 **IRC §72 — MEC Test (Modified Endowment Contract)**
If a policy is overfunded within the first 7 years ("seven-pay test"), it becomes a MEC. MEC treatment eliminates tax-free loans and partial surrenders — a significant client harm. AI monitors premium levels against the 7-pay limit in real time.

## APPENDIX B — Glossary of Terms

**STP (Straight-Through Processing):** Automated approval pathway where an application meets all criteria for approval without any manual underwriting intervention. Requires AI STP Score ≥90.

**MIB (Medical Information Bureau):** An industry-owned database that tracks prior insurance applications. When you apply for life insurance anywhere in North America, certain health information codes are shared with MIB. Used by underwriters to verify disclosure accuracy.

**MVR (Motor Vehicle Report):** A driving record check showing violations, DUIs, suspensions, and accidents for the prior 3–7 years.

**APS (Attending Physician Statement):** A formal medical report requested directly from a treating physician. Required for applications with significant medical history or coverage amounts above certain thresholds.

**MEC (Modified Endowment Contract):** A life insurance policy that has been overfunded beyond IRS 7-pay limits. MEC policies lose favorable tax treatment on withdrawals and loans.

**NIGO (Not In Good Order):** An application returned by the carrier because required information or documents are missing or incomplete.

**CVAT / GPT:** Cash Value Accumulation Test / Guideline Premium Test — two IRS tests that a policy must pass to qualify as life insurance under IRC §7702.

**Reg 60:** New York DFS regulation requiring specific disclosures whenever existing life insurance is being replaced or modified.

**Reg 74:** New York DFS regulation establishing standards for life insurance policy illustrations.

**Reg 187:** New York DFS regulation establishing the Best Interest standard for life insurance and annuity recommendations (effective 2020).

**Per Stirpes:** Beneficiary designation where a deceased beneficiary's share passes to their descendants. Example: If a child beneficiary dies before the insured, their children receive the share.

**Per Capita:** Beneficiary designation where only living named beneficiaries share the death benefit. If one beneficiary dies, the remaining beneficiaries divide the entire benefit.

**UTMA/UGMA:** Uniform Transfers to Minors Act / Uniform Gifts to Minors Act — legal framework for holding assets on behalf of minor beneficiaries until they reach adulthood.

**ILIT (Irrevocable Life Insurance Trust):** A trust that owns a life insurance policy, keeping the death benefit out of the insured's taxable estate.
