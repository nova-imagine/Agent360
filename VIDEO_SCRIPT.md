**[SCREENSHOT: Claims AI Agents overview — showing the six agent categories in a workflow diagram]**

**VOICEOVER:**

> claims lifecycle — not as replacements for your existing workflows,
> but as intelligent augmentation that makes them faster, more accurate,

>

---

### 3A — CLAIMS INTAKE AGENT
**[SCREENSHOT: Claims Intake agent interface — document upload, OCR extraction, structured output visible]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "The Claims Intake Agent transforms the first notification of loss
> from a manual, error-prone process into an intelligent, automated one.
>
> Whether the claim arrives as a PDF, a fax-to-digital scan, an EDI
> transaction, or a web form submission — the Intake Agent reads,
> classifies, and structures every field automatically.
>
> Missing information is flagged with specific, actionable requests.
> Duplicate submissions are detected immediately.
> High-complexity claims are prioritized for fast-track human review.
>
> The result: a 70% reduction in intake processing time,
> and a complete, validated intake record ready for triage
> before a human analyst ever touches it."

---

### 3B — CLAIMS TRIAGE AGENT
**[SCREENSHOT: Claims Triage agent — risk scoring dashboard, priority queue, routing logic visible]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Not all claims are equal. The Triage Agent knows the difference.
>
> Every incoming claim is scored across four dimensions:
> complexity, urgency, fraud risk, and benefit impact.
> 
> Straightforward claims are routed to automated adjudication.
> Complex claims are escalated to specialized reviewers.
> High-fraud-risk claims are flagged for the SIU team.
> Urgent claims — terminal diagnoses, imminent benefit exhaustion —
> are immediately prioritized.
>
> This intelligent routing means your best analysts are always
> working on the cases where human judgment matters most —
> and never wasting time on the ones that don't need them."

---

### 3C — CLAIMS ADJUDICATION AGENT
**[SCREENSHOT: Claims Adjudication agent — policy coverage check, benefit calculation, decision audit trail]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "The Adjudication Agent is where the Lakehouse's full intelligence
> converges on a single decision.
>
> Drawing from the Carrier 360 business rules, the Claimant 360
> coverage record, and the Provider 360 network data —
> the agent validates coverage, calculates the correct benefit amount,
> applies elimination periods and benefit maximums, and produces
> a fully documented adjudication recommendation.
>
> Every decision includes a complete reasoning chain:
> which policy provision applied, which data supported the conclusion,
> and what the confidence level is — all logged for audit."

**[SCREENSHOT: Adjudication agent — showing a decision output with supporting evidence]**

---

### 3D — BUSINESS RULES AGENT
**[SCREENSHOT: Business Rules agent — rule library, carrier-specific rule sets, override logic]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Business rules in LTC are extraordinarily complex —
> carrier-specific, state-specific, product-specific,
> and constantly evolving.
>
> The Business Rules Agent maintains a living library of
> adjudication rules, automatically updated as carriers
> modify their products and as state regulations change.
>
> Rules are expressed in plain language, versioned, and testable —
> so compliance teams can audit them directly, without needing
> to understand the underlying code."

---

### 3E — FRAUD DETECTION AGENT
**[SCREENSHOT: Fraud Detection agent — anomaly scores, suspicious pattern flags, network visualization]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "LTC fraud is sophisticated. Cookie-cutter rules don't catch it.
>
> The Fraud Detection Agent combines statistical anomaly detection
> with knowledge graph traversal to identify patterns that
> rule-based systems miss entirely.
>
> Provider billing outliers. Impossible care timelines.
> Claimant-provider relationships that appear in multiple suspicious claims.
> Coordinated fraud rings that span carriers.
>
> The agent doesn't just flag individual claims — it identifies
> structural fraud patterns across the entire network,
> surfacing them before they become material losses."

---

### 3F — ASSESSMENTS AGENT
**[SCREENSHOT: Assessments agent — functional assessment scoring, care level recommendation, ADL/IADL matrix]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Benefit eligibility in LTC hinges on functional assessments —
> the evaluation of Activities of Daily Living, cognitive status,
> and care level requirements.
>
> The Assessments Agent reads clinical documentation, standardizes
> assessment formats across different providers and tools,
> and produces a consistent, auditable eligibility determination.
>
> It also detects assessment drift — when a claimant's reported
> functional status changes in ways inconsistent with their
> clinical trajectory — a key signal for both care management
> and fraud prevention."

**[SCREENSHOT: Assessments agent — showing ADL matrix and care level output]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Six agents. One continuous workflow. All infusing intelligence
> directly into your existing Illumifin claims operations —
> without disrupting what already works."
